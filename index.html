HOW TO PLAY

1. MOVE

Slide mouse, swipe screen, or use A / D / Arrow Keys to position your paddle.

2. RETURN

Your 3D hand assisted-swings when the ball touches the paddle. Keep eye on the ball!

3. POWER SHOT

Hold Space on keyboard or touch-and-hold on mobile to charge a lightning power smash.

4. TOURNAMENT RULES

First to 11 points (win by 2). Keep the rally going to set new personal bests!
